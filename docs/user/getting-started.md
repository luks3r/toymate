# Getting started with Toymate

Toymate connects your Lumiverse chats to toys supported by [Buttplug.io](https://buttplug.io), through [Intiface Central](https://intiface.com/central) running on the same device as your browser.

## Connect

1. Install Intiface Central, pair your toys there, and press **Start Server**.
2. In Lumiverse, open the **Toymate** tab in the sidebar and press **Connect**. Your toys appear on the **Devices** tab. If one is missing, press the scan button next to Disconnect.
3. Before anything else, calibrate each toy: on **Devices**, open **Calibrate**, set **Floor** to the lowest level you can feel and **Ceiling** to the strongest you want, and try them with the test buttons. Toymate never goes above the ceiling or the **Max level** in **Settings**.

Toymate starts with conservative limits. Raise them only after you know how your toys feel.

## Stop

- **STOP** at the top of the Toymate tab, **Shift+Esc** anywhere, or **Toymate: stop all devices** in the chat input bar's Extras menu. All three stop every toy and pause Toymate until you press **Resume**.
- Send your safeword as the whole message or out of character, for example `(red)`. Everything stops and the chat leaves Story mode. `(yellow)` halves the intensity instead. You can change both words in **Settings**.
- Stopping a generation, disconnecting Intiface, closing the tab, or switching chats also stops everything.

## Choose how a chat drives your toys

Each chat has a mode, chosen at the top of the **Play** tab. Turn on **Use for every chat with this character** to make it that character's default.

- **Off** – nothing happens automatically. You can still play by hand from the **Play** tab.
- **Reactive** – your rules on the **Rules** tab decide: for example, "when the character's reply mentions *kiss*, play a slow wave at 40". **Stream pulse** makes toys pulse while a reply is being written.
- **Story** – after each reply, a separate model call reads it and plans cues that play as you read. Your messages stay clean. Pick the planner's connection in **Settings**; each reply is sent to that provider, so use the one you already roleplay with or a local model. The cue list on **Play** shows each cue next to its sentence and highlights the one playing now. Press ▶ on any cue to play the reply again from there, for example after STOP or when you go back and re-read. That also lifts a pause.

Only new replies move your toys. Opening an old chat, scrolling, or flipping through earlier swipes never replays anything on its own.

## Zones

Group your toys' outputs into zones (for example `clit, internal, stroker`) on the **Devices** tab. Rules and Story mode address zones, never device names. Use **Play by hand** to play a feel (steady, pulse, wave, tease, build, throb, random) at a tempo and level on a zone, or add a short accent (spike, pause, flutter). The **More / Less / Hold / Stop** reactions change the scene right away, and in Story mode the next plan takes them into account.
