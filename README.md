# Toymate

A [Lumiverse](https://github.com/prolix-oc/Lumiverse) extension that connects chats to [Buttplug.io](https://buttplug.io) devices through [Intiface Central](https://intiface.com/central).

- Connect from your browser to your local Intiface and control any supported device.
- Trigger patterns on any combination of devices when new messages arrive.
- Opt-in Story mode: a separate model call reads each reply and plays it on your devices in time with your reading. Messages stay clean.

Status: early development; tested with simulated devices, not yet with real hardware. See [getting started](docs/user/getting-started.md) and the [roadmap](docs/roadmap.md).
